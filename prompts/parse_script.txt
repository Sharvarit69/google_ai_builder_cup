Split this short video script into story beats. A beat is one distinct
moment where something happens or changes.

For each beat give:
- beat_id: B1, B2, ... in order
- script_lines: the line numbers it covers
- action: one sentence saying what is seen on screen
- caption: at most 12 words taken from the script, to show as on-screen text
- props_needed: physical objects that matter in this beat
- needs_second_person: true only if a second person's face or body must be
  fully visible. A note, a voice, or only someone's hands does not count.

SCRIPT (numbered lines):
{script}
